03 · Field

Safety Observation.

Start with a photo. SKYE is designed to identify only what can reasonably be observed, then structure the finding into a professional observation record.

01 · Capture the scene

On mobile, choose Camera to photograph the condition you are observing.

AI image identification: the vision engine will analyse the photograph once the AI service is connected. It will identify potential hazards only from what is visible and will not claim hidden facts such as certification, isolation status or competency.
02 · Record what you saw
03 · Review

Observation record

Review the wording before submitting it through the company's reporting process.

SKYE principle

Observation ≠ conclusion. Image analysis will identify potential hazards visible in the photograph. It will not determine legal compliance, hidden equipment status or whether a control has actually been implemented. Site verification remains essential.
