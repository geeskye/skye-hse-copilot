Review principle

SKYE is a drafting and review tool. The controlled TRA form requires job steps, hazards, initial and revised risk ratings, ALARP controls, responsibility, PTW/safety/environmental checks, PICWT acceptance and Area Authority approval. SKYE should make the work easier to prepare — not replace competent assessment or approval.
